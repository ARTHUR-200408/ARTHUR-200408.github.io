Campus build

Cisco Packet Tracer · 25 September to 8 October 2026 · 10-day build-up program

I built a two-campus network from a single switch up to a full design, and kept a day-by-day journal with commands, problems, and fixes.

  • Planned a /24 subnet for every department and /30 subnets for the WAN links.
  • Set up VLANs, trunking across switches, and inter-VLAN routing on a Layer 3 switch, with DHCP for each VLAN.

Identity and access management design for TechCorp

Tata Cybersecurity Analyst job simulation on Forage · solution design proposal · 1 November 2026

For a simulated global company with 150,000 employees in more than 100 countries, I wrote a design proposal covering how accounts are created, changed, and removed, and how access is controlled.

  • Joiner-mover-leaver automation driven by HR records, extended to contractors, partners, customers, and machine identities.
  • Zero Trust access: one identity provider, phishing-resistant MFA, role plus attribute-based authorisation, risk-based conditional access, and just-in-time privileged access.
  • Tied each design choice to business goals and set measurable targets, such as disabling a leaver’s main account in under 15 minutes.
  • Laid out a three-phase roadmap with quick wins in the first six months, plus a table of risks and mitigations.
